# Keep synthesis

This review treats the ten Keeps as source material, not as ten final directions. Each concept is evaluated for the job it performs best and the ingredient worth carrying forward.

## What each Keep is best for

| Keep | Best at | Carry forward | Do not assume |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 · My Make pulse | A balanced default for a returning contributor | Your teams by default, All teams on demand, meetings and recent posts | Its generic hero or long stacked page is the final presentation |
| 03 · Team cards | Understanding one team without visiting several destinations | One card with next meeting, latest post, team home, handbook, and meetings | A wall of identical cards will scale comfortably to every team |
| 05 · Layered home | Giving newcomers an explicit entrance | “New to contributing? Find a place to begin” as a portable block | The rest of the layered homepage needs to survive as a separate direction |
| 06 · Audience views | Making different contributor contexts explicit | Working New contributor, My Make, and All teams states | Every visitor wants to choose a mode before doing anything |
| 10 · Contributor dashboard | Signaling that the page is personal and adjustable | Personal summary plus a visible Customize entry point | Customization must replace the faster inline star interaction |
| 11 · Make newsroom | Making the network feel current and alive | Editorial headline treatment and recent cross-team publishing | A feed alone answers team navigation and handbook needs |
| 12 · Activity matrix | Comparing the same signals across teams quickly | Source-linked meeting, agenda, and update columns | Dense comparison is the best default for newcomers or small screens |
| 13 · Find your way | Routing someone who knows the destination they need | “Where do you want to go?” search plus task shortcuts | Search should replace browsable team structure or work without good indexing |
| 14 · Favorites rail | Keeping important teams and destinations continuously available | Persistent team shortcuts near handbooks and pathways | Favorites and selected teams require separate concepts or states |
| 15 · Team workspaces | Treating each selected team as a usable working area | Detailed team workspace cards combined with shared resources | It is meaningfully different from 03 unless the surrounding page changes |

## Confirmed interaction model

- A star adds or removes a team from **Your teams**.
- **Your teams** is the default returning view.
- **All teams** is available on demand and should reuse the same team-card grammar.
- Selected teams and favorite teams are one underlying set.
- The exact public label remains open: **Your teams**, **My Make**, or **Your selected teams**.
- No AI is required for version one. Meetings, latest posts, handbooks, pathways, and team destinations can be source-linked deterministic data.

## Design axes

The five synthesis directions vary across four axes:

1. **Default object:** person, audience, task, network activity, or configurable modules.
2. **Primary action:** resume work, choose a context, search, scan current activity, or arrange a workspace.
3. **Information density:** spacious cards, contextual panels, search results, editorial feed, or compact dashboard.
4. **Newcomer treatment:** embedded block, dedicated mode, search shortcut, secondary route, or optional module.

## Five synthesis directions

| Direction | Default view | Primary job | Team discovery | Newcomer route | Density | Evidence recipe |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A · My Make workbench | Your starred teams | Resume contribution across several teams | Your teams / All teams toggle | Embedded beginning block | Spacious cards | 01 + 03 + 05 + 10 + 15 |
| B · Audience gateway | My Make mode | Switch deliberately between contributor contexts | New / My Make / All teams tabs | Dedicated New contributor state | Contextual panels | 03 + 05 + 06 + 15 |
| C · Find and follow | Search and task shortcuts | Reach a known team, handbook, meeting, or pathway | Search results with star controls | Search prompt and pathway shortcut | Search-led cards | 03 + 13 + 14 |
| D · 🃏 Network newsroom | Cross-team activity | See what the project is discussing and publishing now | Stories and signals lead to teams | Secondary beginning route | Editorial feed | 09 ingredient + 11 + 12 |
| E · 🃏 Contribution cockpit | Saved personal workspace | Arrange a deterministic cross-team control surface | Persistent team rail and matrix | Optional module | Compact modules | 10 + 12 + 14 + 15 |

Direction D is the inverted wildcard: it assumes personalization should not be the homepage's first idea. Direction E is the no-constraints wildcard: it tests how far deterministic customization can go without AI or external summarization.
